# Loopable Background Music Plan: *The Bicentennial Man*

Use eight recurring, loopable musical cues plus intentional silence. This keeps the philosophical story coherent without competing with narration.

## Shared Suno settings

Use **Custom → Sounds → Type: Loop** when available, or Custom mode with **Instrumental** enabled. Set the BPM and key stated for each cue. Verify every loop boundary and use a 2–4 second equal-power crossfade if required.

Shared **Exclude** field:

```text
vocals, singing, spoken word, choir, lyrics, prominent solo melody, drum kit, cymbals, trailer hits, impacts, risers, dramatic crescendos, abrupt transitions, fade-out, final cadence
```

For the final mix, keep music roughly 18–24 dB below narration and reduce the 1.5–4 kHz range when it competes with speech.

## 1. The Question of Being

**Use for:** Opening surgery, identity, the Three Laws, and later echoes of Andrew's final decision.  
**Settings:** 54 BPM · D minor

```text
Philosophical retro-futurist ambient underscore for a spoken-word audiobook. Cold glassy synthesizer pads, faint clockwork pulse, isolated felt-piano notes, restrained low strings, recurring four-note motif D-A-F-E. Calm, clinical, existential and quietly inevitable. Seamless 120-second loop, stable low intensity, sparse arrangement, no intro, no outro, no climax, unresolved harmony, beginning and ending with the same texture.
```

## 2. Wooden Hands

**Use for:** Andrew's early family life, creativity, carving, affection, and discovery.  
**Settings:** 64 BPM · D major with suspended harmony

```text
Warm intimate science-fiction ambience for a spoken-word audiobook. Soft felt piano, muted wooden mallets, gentle analog pad, delicate plucked strings and a subtle four-note motif D-A-F-sharp-E. Curious, innocent, handcrafted and affectionate, suggesting creativity awakening inside a machine. Seamless 120-second loop, minimal melody, soft attacks, constant dynamics, no dramatic development, no intro or ending.
```

## 3. Freedom

**Use for:** Andrew asking for freedom, the court decision, his new home, clothing, and the death of Mr. Martin.  
**Settings:** 60 BPM · D Dorian

```text
Restrained hopeful ambient underscore about dignity and personal freedom. Felt piano, soft sustained strings, warm analog synthesizer and a barely audible steady pulse. Bittersweet rather than triumphant, quietly courageous, with warmth shadowed by the passage of time. Seamless 120-second loop, unresolved circular harmony, low melodic density, stable dynamics, no climax, no heroic fanfare, no intro or outro.
```

## 4. The Hostile Street

**Use for:** Social prejudice, humiliation, coercion, and physical danger.  
**Settings:** 56 BPM · D minor

```text
Dark restrained tension ambience for spoken narration. Low synthetic drone, subtle metallic resonance, irregular muted pulse and distant processed breaths of texture. Vulnerable, socially threatening and uncomfortable, but never action-heavy or horrifying. Seamless 90-second loop, very sparse, quiet controlled dynamics, no jump scares, no percussion hits, no crescendo, no resolution, identical atmosphere at beginning and end.
```

## 5. A Voice in Law

**Use for:** The robot history, Amanda's campaign, legal rights, publishing, and confrontations with the company.  
**Settings:** 62 BPM · A minor

```text
Thoughtful civic and legal science-fiction ambient underscore. Muted piano, restrained chamber strings, soft analog arpeggio and subtle clocklike texture. Intelligent, patient, principled and quietly determined, suggesting arguments accumulating across decades. Seamless 120-second loop, unobtrusive beneath speech, repeating harmonic cycle, constant moderate-low energy, no victory climax, no intro, no outro.
```

## 6. Organic Machine

**Use for:** Andrew's artificial-organ research, bodily replacement, laboratories, and transformation.  
**Settings:** 58 BPM · E minor

```text
Bio-mechanical ambient science-fiction underscore for an audiobook. Granular synthesizer pads, organic cello harmonics, soft filtered electrical pulses, tiny clinical clicks and slow breathing-like swells. The boundary between machine and living tissue is becoming uncertain. Curious, clinical and slightly uncanny, never sinister. Seamless 120-second loop, stable texture, no lead melody, no rhythmic build, no climax or ending.
```

## 7. The Long Petition

**Use for:** Andrew's return, political struggle, and refusal to abandon recognition as a human.  
**Settings:** 60 BPM · D minor

```text
Solemn determined ambient underscore about a patient struggle lasting many years. Low warm strings, soft piano ostinato, distant analog pad and restrained heartbeat-like pulse. Dignified, weary and persistent, carrying pain without melodrama. Seamless 120-second loop, circular unresolved chord progression, stable low dynamics, narration-friendly, no heroic climax, no trailer sound, no intro or outro.
```

## 8. Two Hundred Years

**Use for:** Andrew's recognition as human, physical decline, final comfort, and memory of the “Little Miss.”  
**Settings:** 48 BPM · D minor with suspended major warmth

```text
Intimate elegiac ambient underscore for the peaceful ending of a philosophical science-fiction story. Very soft felt piano, warm cello harmonics, delicate analog air and the recurring four-note motif D-A-F-E, now slower and gentler. Mortal, tender, exhausted and quietly fulfilled, sad without sentimentality. Seamless 120-second loop with suspended unresolved harmony, extremely sparse, no swell, no final cadence, no fade-out, beginning and ending in the same breathing texture.
```
